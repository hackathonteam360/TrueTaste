# TrueTaste — Mobile Application UI/UX Design Master Prompt Design a complete **mobile-first restaurant discovery and review application** called **TrueTaste**. The application is a hackathon-ready AI-powered food discovery platform where users discover restaurants, search for specific dishes, scan restaurant QR codes, submit text or voice reviews, receive AI-generated review insights and personalized recommendations, and earn **DineCoins** for their feedback. The design must be optimized specifically for a **mobile smartphone experience** and should feel like a polished real-world startup application. Do NOT design a desktop dashboard or web application in this task. --- # 1. Product Identity ## App Name **TrueTaste** ## Tagline **Real experiences. Smarter recommendations.** Alternative: **Discover food you'll actually love.** ## Core Concept TrueTaste connects: **Restaurant Discovery + Verified Customer Feedback + AI + Rewards** The main product loop is: **Discover → Visit → Scan → Review → Earn → Discover Again** --- # 2. Design Style Create a modern, premium, food-focused mobile application. The design should feel: * Premium * Clean * Friendly * Modern * Trustworthy * AI-powered * Food-focused * Easy to use * Visually engaging The interface should feel similar in quality to modern consumer apps such as: * Airbnb * Uber Eats * Google Maps * Spotify * Duolingo But do NOT copy their UI. Create an original TrueTaste visual identity. --- # 3. Mobile-First Requirements Design specifically for: **390 × 844 px** Primary target: **Modern Android smartphones** The UI must also adapt naturally to: * 360 × 800 * 412 × 915 * iPhone-sized screens Use: * Safe areas * Comfortable touch targets * Bottom navigation * Thumb-friendly controls * Large readable text * Scrollable content * Sticky important CTAs where appropriate Avoid desktop-style layouts. --- # 4. Brand Colors Use a warm food-inspired color system. ### Primary **#FF6B35** Use for: * Primary buttons * Active navigation * Rating highlights * Important CTAs * DineCoin accents ### Dark **#171717** Use for: * Headings * Important text * Navigation elements ### Background **#FAFAF8** ### Secondary Background **#F3F4F1** ### Cards **#FFFFFF** ### Success **#22C55E** ### Warning **#F59E0B** ### Error **#EF4444** ### AI Accent Use a subtle purple/blue accent for AI-specific components. Do not allow the AI accent to overpower the food brand. --- # 5. Typography Use: **Inter** or **Manrope** Recommended hierarchy: ### H1 28–32px Bold ### H2 22–26px Bold/Semibold ### H3 18–20px Semibold ### Body 14–16px ### Caption 12–13px Maintain strong hierarchy and generous spacing. --- # 6. Navigation Use a fixed mobile bottom navigation. Five sections: **Home** **Explore** **Rewards** **Activity** **Profile** Icons: Home → Home icon Explore → Search/Compass Rewards → Coin/Gift Activity → Chart/History Profile → User The active tab should use the primary brand color. --- # 7. Splash Screen Create a minimal premium splash screen. Center: TrueTaste logo Below: **TrueTaste** **Real experiences. Smarter recommendations.** Use subtle food/AI visual elements. Do not overcrowd the screen. --- # 8. Onboarding Create 3 onboarding screens. ## Screen 1 ### Discover food made for you. Explain: **Find restaurants and dishes based on your personal taste.** Visual: Beautiful food photography + restaurant discovery UI. CTA: **Next** --- ## Screen 2 ### Share your experience. Explain: **Scan a restaurant QR code and tell us what you really think.** Visual: Smartphone scanning a QR code at a restaurant table. CTA: **Next** --- ## Screen 3 ### Review. Earn. Enjoy. Explain: **Earn DineCoins when you share your experience and use them for rewards.** Visual: Review → Coins → Reward. CTA: **Get Started** --- # 9. Location / City Selection After onboarding, ask: ### Where are you hungry? Search field: **Search your city...** Popular cities: * Lahore * Islamabad * Karachi * Rawalpindi * Faisalabad * Multan Use clean city cards. Allow: **Use my location** --- # 10. Taste Preferences Create a visually attractive preference setup. Heading: ### Tell us what you love. Cuisine selection: * Pakistani * Italian * Chinese * BBQ * Fast Food * Korean * Mexican * Desserts * Cafe Dish preferences: * Biryani * Karahi * Burger * Pizza * Pasta * Steak * Fried Chicken Spice preference: **Mild** **Medium** **Spicy** **Very Spicy** Budget: **$** **$$** **$$$** **$$$$** CTA: **Build My Taste Profile** --- # 11. HOME SCREEN This should be the strongest screen in the application. Top: **Good evening 👋** Subtitle: **What are you craving today?** Large search bar: **Search restaurants, dishes or cuisines** Include search icon and optional voice-search icon. --- ## Quick Categories Horizontal scroll. EXPLORE SCREEN Header: ### Explore Search: **Search restaurants or dishes...** Filter chips: **Cuisine** **Rating** **Price** **Distance** **Open now** **AI Match** Add toggle: **List | Map** Default to List. AI RECOMMENDATION DETAILS Whenever AI recommends a restaurant, show WHY. Example: ### Why we recommend this **92% Match** ✓ You like Pakistani food ✓ You rated BBQ highly ✓ You prefer spicy dishes ✓ People with similar tastes enjoyed this restaurant This makes AI recommendations understandable and trustworthy. --- # 30. NOTIFICATIONS Create a clean notification screen. Examples: **🎉 You earned 10 DineCoins** **✨ New restaurant recommendation** **🎁 Your reward is ready** **⭐ Someone found your review helpful** --- # 32. EMPTY STATES Create polished empty states. Examples: ### No restaurants found **Try another dish, cuisine or location.** CTA: **Explore nearby** --- ### No reviews yet **Be the first to share your experience.** CTA: **Write a Review** --- ### No rewards yet **Start reviewing restaurants to earn DineCoins.** CTA: **Find a Restaurant** --- # 33. LOADING STATES Use skeleton loading instead of generic spinners. For AI: ### Analyzing your review... Then: **Finding key insights...** Then: **Generating your summary...** Use subtle animation. --- # 34. ERROR STATES Example: ### Something went wrong **We couldn't load restaurants right now.** CTA: **Try Again** Keep errors friendly. --- # 35. COMPONENT SYSTEM Create a consistent reusable design system. Components: * Primary Button * Secondary Button * Search Bar * Restaurant Card * Dish Card * Rating Component * AI Card * Review Card * QR Scanner * Voice Recorder * Coin Card * Reward Card * Subscription Card * Filter Chips * Bottom Navigation * Modal * Toast * Progress Bar * Donut Chart * Skeleton Loader Use consistent: * Border radius * Spacing * Typography * Icon style * Shadows * Button height * Card structure --- # 36. CARD STYLE Use moderately rounded cards. Recommended radius: **16–20px** Avoid excessive floating cards. Use subtle shadows and borders. Cards should feel lightweight. --- # 37. ICON STYLE Use a consistent modern icon family. Prefer: **Lucide-style icons** Icons should be: * Simple * Rounded * Minimal * Consistent Do not mix multiple icon styles. --- # 38. FOOD IMAGERY Use high-quality realistic food photography. Prioritize: * Close-up dishes * Restaurant interiors * Food presentation * Dining experiences * Restaurant ambience Use consistent image ratios. Avoid obviously artificial or low-quality stock photography. --- # 39. AI VISUAL LANGUAGE AI features should have a recognizable but subtle visual identity. Use: ✨ sparkle icon Small AI badges Subtle gradients Soft glow Animated processing indicators But do not turn the entire application into an "AI-looking" interface. AI should feel like a useful product capability rather than decoration. --- # 40. DineCoin Visual Language Create a recognizable DineCoin icon. Concept: A premium digital coin containing: **TT** or a subtle fork/spoon symbol. Use this consistently across: * Rewards * Review success * Transactions * Profile * Notifications * Subscription --- # 41. MICROINTERACTIONS Add subtle mobile animations: * Heart favorite animation * Button press feedback * QR scan success animation * DineCoin reward animation * Review submission success * AI processing animation * Voice waveform * Filter selection * Bottom navigation transitions * Restaurant image transitions Animations should be fast and polished. --- # 42. ACCESSIBILITY Ensure: * Strong text contrast * Minimum readable font sizes * Large touch targets * Clear active/inactive states * Icons do not rely exclusively on color * Voice functionality has text alternative * Important actions are easy to reach with one hand --- # 43. COMPLETE USER JOURNEY Design the screens so this journey feels natural: Splash ↓ Onboarding ↓ City Selection ↓ Taste Preferences ↓ Home ↓ Search ↓ Dish Search ↓ Restaurant Detail ↓ AI Review Summary ↓ QR Scanner ↓ Review ↓ Voice Review ↓ AI Processing ↓ Review Submitted ↓ DineCoins Earned ↓ Rewards ↓ AI Recommendations ↓ Profile The main product loop is: **Discover → Visit → Review → Earn → Learn → Recommend** --- # 44. PRIMARY MVP SCREENS If time is limited, prioritize these screens first: 1. Splash 2. Onboarding 3. Home 4. Search 5. Restaurant Detail 6. QR Scanner 7. Review 8. Voice Review 9. AI Review Summary 10. Review Success 11. DineCoins 12. Rewards 13. Profile These screens must be highly polished because they represent the main hackathon demo. --- # 45. FINAL DESIGN OBJECTIVE The final TrueTaste mobile application should look like a **real consumer startup product**, not a basic hackathon prototype. The experience should immediately communicate: ### DISCOVER Find restaurants and dishes you'll love. ### TRUST Learn from real customer experiences. ### AI Get recommendations and review insights personalized to you. ### REWARD Earn DineCoins for sharing your experience. The final visual experience should be: **Premium + Simple + Food-focused + AI-powered + Reward-driven** Most importantly, prioritize **clarity and usability over visual complexity**. The application should be easy enough for a first-time user to understand within 10 seconds.
